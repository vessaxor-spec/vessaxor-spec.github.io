06 / DESIGN DISCIPLINE

Architecture is also what the system refuses to do.

01

The model is not the architecture.

Intelligence should be replaceable without making the surrounding system disposable.

02

Competence does not imply authority.

Knowing how to act does not grant permission to act.

03

Evidence before confidence.

Plausibility matters. Observable proof matters more.

04

Failure must not widen power.

Fallback, retry, recovery, and replanning must preserve authority boundaries.

05

Continuity is infrastructure.

State, memory, recovery, and provenance should survive beyond a model session.

06

Evolution must re-earn trust.

More capable systems should not silently become less governed.
