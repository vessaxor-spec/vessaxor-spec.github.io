02 / ORIENTATION

Systems that keep their rules when the model changes.

VESSAXOR is a public systems lab focused on the parts of AI operation that should survive model releases: responsibility, authority, evidence, verification, persistence, and recovery.

WHAT THIS IS

Architecture for governed AI work.

TEO governs how work is assigned and verified. GroX turns human intent into bounded, persistent execution.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not a model leaderboard or autonomous-authority claim.

Models are implementations inside a larger system. Capability does not grant permission, and experiments do not become operational claims by implication.

WHY IT MATTERS

Models move faster than governance should.

A durable system should be able to change models without silently changing who may act, how evidence is produced, or what happens when execution fails.

06 / DESIGN DISCIPLINE

Architecture is also what the system refuses to do.

Six durable constraints govern the work. Expand them when the philosophy matters; skip them when you only need the systems.

Read the six principles ↓
  1. 01

    The model is not the architecture.

    Intelligence should be replaceable without making the surrounding system disposable.

  2. 02

    Competence does not imply authority.

    Knowing how to act does not grant permission to act.

  3. 03

    Evidence before confidence.

    Plausibility matters. Observable proof matters more.

  4. 04

    Failure must not widen power.

    Fallback, retry, recovery, and replanning must preserve authority boundaries.

  5. 05

    Continuity is infrastructure.

    State, memory, recovery, and provenance should survive beyond a model session.

  6. 06

    Evolution must re-earn trust.

    More capable systems should not silently become less governed.
