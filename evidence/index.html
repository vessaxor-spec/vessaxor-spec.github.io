03 / METHOD

A claim is stronger when its boundary is visible.

Evidence is interpreted narrowly: tests prove the checks they actually executed; controlled experiments remain experiments; staged capability remains staged; missing evidence cannot be converted into confidence.

THE PATTERN

Claim → Evidence → Boundary → Primary source.

This pattern is now a public design primitive across VESSAXOR. It makes system status legible without turning the portfolio into a marketing layer that outranks the repositories.

THE RULE

Implementation is not completion.

Where a claim is consequential, the public surface should make it possible to inspect what evidence exists, what it does not prove, and where the authoritative record lives.
